Vased Arrangements & Full Events

Vased Arrangements

Pricing varies based on your request — flower selection, size, and vase all play a part. If you'd like to provide your own vase, just let me know; otherwise a vase fee applies. Tell me your vision (a Pinterest board is perfect) and I'll put together a quote.

Weddings & Events

From bridal bouquets to full ceremony and reception florals, I've had the joy of designing for full-service weddings. Every event is quoted individually based on your date, venue, and vision — reach out and let's start planning.

Please note: delivery fees are not included in the pricing above and are quoted separately based on location.
